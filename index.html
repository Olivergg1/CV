Volvo IT Summer intern [Summer 2023]

Summer intern at Volvo IT in Skövde during the summer of 2023. Worked in a team of 5 interns, within the responsible team of ordinary employees. Our task through out the summer was to begin the refactoration and redesign of an internal system. We worked with an agil team collaboration framwork (SCRUM) where the main focus was weekly feedback, planning and weekly goals. The technologies used were C#, both in frontend (Blazor + Fluxor) and backend (.NET). Microsoft Azure was also used as a managment medium for backlogs and scrum planning. My time at Volvo enhanced my skills in programming, practicaly but also in terms of collaboration and communication within the team. It was a good oppurtunity to work with SCRUM, after only having read about it in University courses.

UNGATIO designcamp [Summer 2019]

Two weeks of designing and problem solving. A group of ten students from schools in Östergötland gathered together to solve several problems and request from companies such as Linköping Airport, Mjölby kommun, Hallins and more. During these weeks, visits were made to the different companies' facilities, where we got tours and had discussions with the staff, inorder to gather more close-to-life information and inspiration. The information gathered was then used to develop solutions for the different problems. After the period of 2 weeks, staff from all companies was invited and gathered for a final presentation where the solutions were presented.

For more details, please visit UNGATIO's website.
